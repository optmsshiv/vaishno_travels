✦ The Four Sacred Shrines ✦

The Char Dhams of Uttarakhand

The Char Dham pilgrimage is considered one of the holiest journeys a Hindu can undertake. Completing all four shrines is believed to cleanse all sins and bestow moksha.

01
Yamunotri
Source of River Yamuna · 3,293 m

The westernmost shrine of the Char Dham circuit — dedicated to Goddess Yamuna. Pilgrims trek 6 km from Janki Chatti to reach the temple and sacred hot water kunds. Bathing in the Surya Kund and offering rice and potatoes to the deity is the chief ritual.

02
Gangotri
Source of River Ganga · 3,048 m

The temple is dedicated to Goddess Ganga at the site where the sacred river first descended to Earth from Lord Shiva's matted locks. The shrine is surrounded by peaks of the Gangotri glacier. Bathing at Bhagirathi Shila is considered deeply purifying.

03
Kedarnath
Lord Shiva Jyotirlinga · 3,583 m

One of the 12 Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva, Kedarnath is the most challenging of the four dhams. The ancient stone temple stands majestically amidst snow-capped Himalayan peaks. Pilgrims trek 16 km from Gaurikund or opt for helicopter services. The divine energy here is unmatched.

04
Badrinath
Lord Vishnu · 3,133 m

The final and most visited dham — dedicated to Lord Badrinarayan (Vishnu), flanked by Nar and Narayan peaks. The temple sits beside the Alaknanda river. The warm Tapt Kund for bathing and the Brahma Kapal for ancestral rituals make this a complete spiritual experience.

✦ Day-by-Day Journey ✦

13-Day Itinerary

Day 1
Arrival at Haridwar / Rishikesh

Group assembly. Welcome puja at Har Ki Pauri ghat. Ganga Aarti darshan. Hotel check-in and briefing. Rest for the journey ahead.

Day 2
Haridwar → Barkot (Yamunotri base)

Drive through the scenic Yamuna valley. Check-in at Barkot. Preparation for Yamunotri trek.

Day 3
Yamunotri Darshan

Trek 6 km to Yamunotri temple. Holy dip in Surya Kund. Puja at Yamunotri shrine. Return to Barkot.

Day 4
Barkot → Uttarkashi

Drive to Uttarkashi — the City of Shiva. Evening aarti at Vishwanath temple. Overnight stay.

Day 5
Gangotri Darshan

Drive to Gangotri (3,048 m). Darshan of Goddess Ganga. Sacred bath at Bhagirathi Shila. Return to Uttarkashi.

Day 6–7
Uttarkashi → Kedarnath

Drive to Gaurikund via Guptkashi. Night stay. Next day: 16 km trek or helicopter to Kedarnath. VIP darshan of Lord Shiva. Overnight at Kedarnath for early morning rudrabhishek.

Day 8
Kedarnath → Badrinath

Descend to Gaurikund. Drive via Ukhimath to Badrinath. Evening aarti at Badrinath temple.

Day 9
Badrinath Darshan

Early morning abhishek. Tapt Kund bath. Brahma Kapal puja for ancestors. Mana village visit — last village before China border.

Day 10–13
Return Journey

Badrinath → Rudraprayag → Devprayag → Rishikesh → Haridwar. Final Ganga snan. Group prasad distribution. Departure from Haridwar / Dehradun airport.
