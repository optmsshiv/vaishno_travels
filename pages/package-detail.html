Experience the eternal city of Lord Shiva in all its divine glory. This 5-day package covers the most sacred ghats, the magnificent Kashi Vishwanath Temple, and the unforgettable evening Ganga Aarti — a spiritual experience that stays with you for life.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1
Arrival in Varanasi

Pick-up from Varanasi station / airport. Hotel check-in. Evening: Dashashwamedh Ghat Ganga Aarti — a divine welcome to Kashi. Dinner and overnight stay.

Day 2
Kashi Vishwanath Temple

Early morning: VIP darshan at Shri Kashi Vishwanath Jyotirlinga. Rudrabhishek puja. Visit Annapurna Devi temple and Kaal Bhairav temple. Boat ride at sunrise on the Ganga.

Day 3
Sarnath Excursion

Day trip to Sarnath — where Lord Buddha delivered his first sermon. Visit Dhamek Stupa and Sarnath Museum. Evening: Manikarnika Ghat visit (the eternal cremation ground).

Day 4
Ghats Walk & Puja

Morning walk along 84 ghats of Varanasi. Panchamrit abhishek puja. Visit Tulsi Manas Mandir and Durga Kund. Free evening for shopping on Vishwanath Lane.

Day 5
Departure with Blessings

Final Ganga snan and puja. Prasad distribution. Drop to station / airport. Carry the blessings of Kashi home.
