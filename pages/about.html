✦ Our Journey ✦

Born from Devotion, Built on Trust

Shri Vaishnо Travels was founded in 2012 by a family of devout pilgrims from Patna, Bihar, with a simple mission: to make sacred yatra accessible, safe, and spiritually fulfilling for every devotee — regardless of age or physical ability.

What began as a small group of 15 pilgrims to Varanasi has grown into a trusted yatra company serving over 15,000 devotees across India. We have guided families, senior citizens, solo pilgrims, and large groups to every major shrine in the country.

Our strength lies in our personal touch — we treat every pilgrim as a family member and every yatra as our own. Each trip is meticulously planned so you can focus entirely on your devotion while we handle every earthly detail.
